## Root causes found and fixed (all deployed to production)

1. **Event-loop stalls → Fly proxy cut all routing** (killed 8 turns): streaming grounding
   validators re-scanned every governed cell per commentary/reasoning-summary event (≤48/turn).
   Fixed by per-result evidence caching (80ms→5ms/event, commit 97695c7) + performance-CPU VM +
   10s livez timeout.
2. **Abandoned turns locked conversations** ("another turn is already running", killed 4 turns +
   real-owner follow-ups after any crashed turn): `begin_albert_turn` ignored lease expiry.
   Migration **0177** settles expired-lease running turns; eval runner sweeps its own orphans.
3. **One transient network blip killed healthy turns** (6 turns): the service client never
   retried a severed poll though jobs survive server-side and polls are cursor-based. Client now
   retries transients with backoff (~24s); duplicate background-job submits re-attach (202) while
   the job is alive.
4. **Scenario arithmetic was unsayable** (2 refusals + goal-tier dodges): derive_result had no
   scaling. New `scale` expression (owner-stated factor, disclosed in formula/notes) + prompt rule:
   estimate with a named basis, never refuse. Verified: 30% clearance now yields $4,942.88/$1,931.99
   with per-item detail.
5. **Recipe fast-path misroutes** ("Explain working capital…" → "Today's roster has 1 shift."):
   uncovered question words were ignored unless another recipe claimed them. Coverage gate: ≥3
   unknown content words fail open to the full runtime; analytical verbs bail.
6. **~1.6 wasted model round-trips per turn**: plan-step saturation now auto-advances (80
   bounces/run), limit>500 clamps instead of rejecting (12/run).
7. **missed_facet (52/59 judged fails)**: briefs now enumerate the owner's coordinated
   sub-questions as concrete `answerMustCover` requirements (deterministic clause extraction;
   contract caps raised 6×240→10×300 after the old cap rejected inflated briefs). Subset result:
   4 v2-fails flipped to pass, incl. H-24's "10h × $26.05 → monthly saving" class.
