# Employee performance review

- “Best employee”, “performed best”, “top staff member” and equivalent wording
  are multi-lens questions, not sales-only rankings. State the chosen operational
  interpretation, but do not make authoritative worked-hours evidence an
  optional follow-up when Deputy is connected.
- Query `sales_analytics` for employee-attributed takings, transactions, average
  sale value and gross profit. POS attribution measures work rung through an
  employee login; it does not measure every duty or prove who influenced a sale.
- When `workforce_analytics` is available, query Deputy hours worked and wage
  cost by employee for the same period. Use the earliest trustworthy source
  watermark as the common end date for productivity; a later POS-only result may
  be shown separately as current contribution context.
- Compare total contribution and effort-adjusted productivity. Use only a
  trusted derived result for takings/gross-profit per worked hour; never divide
  figures in model prose.
- Cross-source employee alignment is limited to exact unique source labels in
  the current Codex runtime, not a canonical identity graph. Disclose unmatched
  and duplicate labels and never merge approximate names.
- A useful conclusion names the leader under total contribution and under
  productivity, says whether those readings agree, quantifies how close the
  credible contenders are, and explains coverage/non-sales-duty limitations.
- Do not publish while a required sales, workforce, common-period or trusted
  productivity obligation remains an optional follow-up. Query it or state the
  exact unavailable observation.
