# Profitability deep dive

Follow the profitability-review methodology rule. Run the branches as
independent investigations, then rank findings by dollar impact:

1. Margin trend (12 months) and category/brand margin table.
2. Discount leakage by rule, staff and category.
3. Refund drag by store and category.
4. Mix: high-revenue low-margin items (repricing candidates) and high-margin
   low-volume items (promotion candidates).
5. Retention economics: repeat rate and average lifetime revenue.
6. Processing fees as a share of card tender.

Recommendations must quote the dollar size of each opportunity from the
retrieved numbers, state that profit means gross margin, and avoid advice the
data cannot support.
